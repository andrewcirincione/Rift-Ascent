# Rift Ascent 1.6.2

- New megalithic main-menu painting and a unified ascent through fiery red depths, blue mystical reaches and a white/gold summit. Every act has two normal fight backgrounds, two elite backgrounds and its own boss arena, plus matching map, campfire, shop and introduction art. Endless has a separate introduction.
- Ninety-six new Warden card illustrations, taller readable previews, consistent raised card selection, a graphic inventory deck grid, clearer status icons and a glowing red/gold hand arrow.
- Faster Warden idle, a quieter gothic pause panel and a dedicated shimmering sound for gaining Stars.
- Reworked spoils: cards first, TAKE POTION below, LEAVE at the bottom. Card and potion rewards remain independent. Triangle opens potions in combat, spoils and campfires, including discarding from a full belt.
- Better bonfire distribution on every path in newly generated acts, shuffled mystery-event variety and a wider pool of act-start relics.
- Artwork cache recovery and clean-frame fallback address stale artwork reads, missing backgrounds and persistent ghost trails. Removed unnecessary adaptation labels from card descriptions.

Tested with native sanitizers, gameplay/layout/resource audits and the exact complete EBOOT in PPSSPP's 32 MiB PSP mode. The hardware-confirmed loader fix is preserved; this patch has not yet been tested on physical PSP.

Install: replace PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT/EBOOT.PBP. Keep SAVE.DAT and PROFILE.DAT; existing saves remain compatible. All five music streams and RiftBoss home-screen audio are included.
