# Rift Ascent 1.8.6-dist2 — additional IP review and text differentiation

This prerelease strengthens the distribution edition's independent identity.
It is not legal clearance or assurance against a cease-and-desist. Read
`LEGAL_REVIEW.md` before planning wider distribution: public development builds,
an unrelated game's use of the same title, and provenance/presentation questions
remain unresolved. Existing branches and published releases have been preserved.

- Reworked Proxy's story around a constructed witness, erased testimony and the
  rift's falsified records. Removed the Regent/Sovereign Blade identity remnants
  across entry scenes, boss exchanges, events, return dialogue and endings.
- Removed retired Sly/Inky UI instructions and badges. Reworded a reward heading
  and enemy intent labels; renamed Abyss Watcher to Hollow Surveyor.
- Corrected character-selection HP text to the existing 78/73/76 starting values
  and made the Journey Forge gift text identify the Twinwoven modifier correctly.
- Added a pinned comparison against 2,212 Slay the Spire 1/2 reference rows:
  no exact normalized matches for 514 collectible names, no full-description
  matches and no shared eight-word source-string sequence. These finite checks
  are regression screening, not a legal similarity test.
- Included a substantive IP review with primary legal/policy sources, a recent
  Mega Crit enforcement example, exact scope, remaining risks and a counsel
  handoff. Artwork and music remain unchanged and outside provenance review,
  as directed by the owner.

All 14,700 balance simulations were rerun: campaign outcomes and card-choice
CSVs are byte-identical to dist1. Native sanitizer, progression, UI and full
ending-timeline suites pass, including 2,723 story-text samples. The complete
PSP executable was checked in PPSSPP for combat/forge behavior, character gates,
revised story scenes and the individual/shared ending. See `VALIDATION.md` for
the exact coverage and limitations. Physical PSP hardware was not available.

Card/relic/potion rules, difficulty, save layout, five-win secret access,
ten-win unlocks and the once-only shared-finale condition are unchanged.
Install into `PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT_DIST`; dist1 distribution saves remain usable.
The in-game version remains 1.8.6. The complete soundtrack is included.
