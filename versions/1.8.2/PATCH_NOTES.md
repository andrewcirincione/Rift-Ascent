# Rift Ascent 1.8.2

- Added subtle, softened silhouette shadows behind player characters and enemies. They follow the existing animation art; no regenerated shadow images.
- Reworked 27 enemy illustrations for stronger silhouettes, contrast and act identity. Each of the first three acts has ten normal species and four distinct elites; later acts mix these pools. Added Crown Eater as Act 3's fourth unique elite.
- Added six clearly telegraphed elite/boss abilities: Fracture, Sear, Rally, Purge, Gloom and Blight. Intent text and the combat log explain their effects.
- Replaced the lingering partial-screen transition veil with a full-screen fade. Normal and elite battle transitions are slightly longer and include a brief music-free interval.
- Swapped the requested themes: RiftFight4 now plays in normal encounters; RiftFight5 now plays in elite encounters.
- Softened the XMB RiftBoss preview entrance, with a short silent lead and clean tail. Preserved all ten full in-game music streams.
- Kept unlock totals in the Unlocks browser, removed them from the main menu, inset save-slot text from its selection frame, and fixed the bright strip on unselected character panels.
- Reduced Forgotten Armory and similarly presented mystery-offer previews to portrait proportions. Preview dimensions elsewhere are unchanged.
- Nameless Exchange now uses the same raised card, selection marker and highlight as other card-choice screens. Upgrade, removal, copy, modification, reward and combat-choice layouts retain that feedback.
- Replaced the oversized multi-enemy target decoration with a smaller, quieter marker and shadow.
- Aligned ordinary card rarity rolls with STS2's base normal/elite/boss/shop bands and rare-card offset. Normal rewards use 60/37/3 base weights, elites 50/40/10, bosses all Rare, and shops 54/37/9. The shared offset changes the actual combat odds as rewards are rolled. Ancient cards remain special-event/story rewards.
- Added 22 mystery rooms (39 total), including single offers and three-way choices: potion trading, permanent Strength, paired upgrades, card changes, relic bargains and a coin gamble. Four additional modification rooms, a potion-for-upgrade trade, card transformation, and saved next-combat status bargains are included.
- Reduced character story encounters to one 1% roll after each completed non-boss map room, after its rewards. Inventory visits cannot produce extra rolls.
- Preserved four save slots, shared account unlocks, secret Act 4 progression, the Act 7 boss, and Endless routing. Existing 1.8 and 1.8.1 run saves migrate to the new version.

The complete EBOOT includes all artwork and the supplied soundtrack. Copy it into your PSP game folder while retaining your ASCENT18 save files.
