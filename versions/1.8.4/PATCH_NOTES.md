# Rift Ascent 1.8.4

- Added Custom Run. Build a starting deck from all three character catalogs, including locked cards, choose quantities and upgrades, and remove copies in Your Deck. Choose starting relics and potions with separate selected-item tabs; the belt still holds at most three potions. Drafts and active custom runs use ordinary save slots marked CUSTOM. They award no unlocks, character wins, act records or ascension records.
- Custom decks support all three characters' mechanics. Up to 128 cards and 96 relic entries can be selected. Duplicate relic pickup bonuses repeat; ongoing passive effects retain their normal behavior.
- Defeated runs are deleted from their save slots. A small receipt prevents an older backup of the same run from reappearing. Account unlocks and character records are preserved.
- Added Normal and Fast combat speed in Options. Fast advances combat animation timing three times as quickly and shortens input windups; map transitions and their quiet music interval retain their timing.
- Selected unlocks and custom-loadout tiles rise above their neighbors and display the same gold selection crest used elsewhere in the interface.
- Warden and Vesper show the Star counter only when they have a card that can grant Stars, including generators in combat piles or active card effects. Proxy always shows it. Custom Warden/Vesper do not receive Proxy's opening Stars unless Divine Right is selected.
- Added a larger original gothic target crest for multi-enemy fights.
- Reduced repeated drawing calculations for scaled artwork, panels and card frames. Pixel comparison checks retain identical visuals, including opacity and clipping.
- Maps use seven possible opening-lane combinations with more varied forks, joins and connections. Imported runs keep their existing current-act routes; new acts use the new layouts.
- Normal and elite enemy pools avoid repeating a species within an act until the available pool has been exhausted. Both enemies in a paired encounter remain distinct.
- A small fraction of nonattacking turns become basic strikes. Some basic strikes instead split their strength between an attack and Block, showing both intent icons and the full description. Enemy HP and damage scaling are unchanged.
- Boss card spoils offer Rare cards with a small chance of an eligible Ancient card. Character unlock requirements still apply. The existing spoils heading is retained.
- Existing mystery choices and Leave options are preserved. Added three occasional adverse rooms: Falling Ossuary, The Empty Tithe and Spore Wake. These require a choice, but always remain resolvable without killing or trapping the player.
- Healing choices remain selectable at full health. Healing is capped at maximum HP and any stated costs or side effects still apply.
- Removed the transient victory/reward notification from the combat-to-spoils presentation. The existing finishing timing remains intact.
- Existing saves migrate with account progress, slot contents, rarity state and event history intact. All ten complete music streams and the splash music are preserved.

Install: replace EBOOT.PBP in PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT. Keep SAVE.DAT, PROFILE.DAT and every ASCENT18_* file so account progress, active slots, settings and retired-run receipts remain intact.
