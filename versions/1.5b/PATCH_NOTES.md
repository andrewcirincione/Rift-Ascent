# Rift Ascent 1.5b — Nightshade

Choose the Warden or the new gothic ninja Nightshade when starting a normal or endless run.

Nightshade brings a separate Silent-based card pool: Shivs, Poison, Sly discard chains, Retain, Burst, Dexterity, Intangible, and the associated powers and card-copy mechanics. She starts with 70 HP, 99 Gold, 3 Energy, a 12-card deck, and Serpent Ring's two extra opening draws.

Includes 89 distinct new card illustrations, a newly generated character model with idle/attack/block/power/death animation sets, dagger attack effects, nine character relics/blessings, and new status emblems. Solo rewards contain 80 regular character cards; Ancient cards are offered as act blessings. Multiplayer-only cards are excluded from solo play.

Existing card modifications work with the new pool, with dynamic damage/Block descriptions and visible Sly/Inky markers. Mandatory discard choices cannot be bypassed through other menus, and Save & Quit preserves the exact pending effect. Legacy Warden saves migrate automatically.

Also fixes Strength reduction across all enemy attack types, stacked Block multipliers, and repeated attacks retargeting after a kill. The complete soundtrack and XMB music are included.

Validated with independent arithmetic tests, legacy regressions, save migration tests, 300 automated runs, layout/animation audits, and the exact EBOOT in PPSSPP. This is the first Nightshade release; automated-run results are calibration evidence rather than a human balance guarantee.

Install `EBOOT.PBP` in `PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT/`. Keep your existing `SAVE.DAT` and `PROFILE.DAT`.
