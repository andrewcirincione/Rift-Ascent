# Rift Ascent 1.7

Warden, Vesper and Proxy now have interwoven stories told through painted gothic scenes on black. Each has ten possible lines when entering each of the three acts, plus ten for Endless. Twelve new entry illustrations follow their origins in the red furnace wards, blue star-court and white/gold summit.

Each character has five possible three-beat exchanges with each act boss. Nine new confrontation illustrations and three matching combat models ensure the Furnace Maw, Crimson Judge and Hollow King are the same adversaries in dialogue and battle. Boss stats and attacks are unchanged. Boss dialogue is limited to the first three acts.

X advances immediately. Scenes fade smoothly through black into the next event; quick presses do not leak into map or combat actions. Entry scenes are silent apart from the X click and lead to the map with RiftMenu. Boss scenes begin RiftBoss, which continues into battle without restarting. Existing act blessings occur before the entry scene. Saving during a dialogue preserves its exact page.

The main menu now offers Resume Run, Begin Ascent and How to Play. Endless remains available after winning a normal run, and existing Endless saves still resume.

The complete soundtrack, animated home-screen icon, splash wallpaper and RiftBoss home-screen audio are preserved. Save format remains v23. The hardware-confirmed loader correction and fixed 4 MiB artwork cache remain in place.

Validated on the exact complete EBOOT in PPSSPP 1.20.4, 32 MiB PSP mode: all scene illustrations and boss fights, fade frames, silent entry scenes, continuous boss audio, held/rapid X, save/quit and cold resume. Native audits cover all 255 dialogue pages, 2,287 identical resident/streamed renders, 6,764 Regent assertions, route/event/blessing regressions and artwork recovery. Physical PSP testing of this patch remains pending.

Install the PSP ZIP at the Memory Stick root, or place the standalone file as `PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT/EBOOT.PBP`. Preserve `SAVE.DAT` and `PROFILE.DAT`.
