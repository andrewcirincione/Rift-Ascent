# Rift Ascent 1.8.6-r4

- The secret fourth act opens on each character's fifth Act 3 win, including that fifth run. The character-selection golden A is awarded at the same five-win milestone.
- Character card unlocks still award two different-rarity cards per Act 3 win through the tenth win: twenty cards per character. Potion and relic progression is unchanged.
- Added **Three as One**, a five-page final scene with new artwork showing Warden, Vesper and Proxy together. It follows the individual Act 4 ending when the third distinct character earns their first Act 4 boss victory, in any character order.
- The shared finale is awarded once per profile. Interrupted viewing resumes from its saved page; completing it prevents replay from older run snapshots or future victories. Custom runs do not award it. Profiles that already defeated Act 4 with all three characters before this update do not receive a retroactive replay.
- Existing save and account layouts, difficulty, journey rewards, boss fades and all twelve soundtrack streams are retained. In-game version remains 1.8.6.

Validation details and reproduction commands are in `qa/v186r4/README.md`. Install the PSP ZIP or replace `PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT/EBOOT.PBP`, keeping all existing save/account files, including the new `ASCENT18_FINALE` files.
