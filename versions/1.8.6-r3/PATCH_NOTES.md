# Rift Ascent 1.8.6-r3

- Added a gentle act-by-act increase in enemy HP and damage above the existing curve, leaving Act 1 unchanged. Returning early-act enemies meet an Act 3 stat floor from Act 4 onward. Boss/minion damage sharing and the earlier 5% boss bonus are retained.
- Later story encounters offer stronger relics and better cards: more Rares and upgrades, with occasional unlocked Ancients in later ascents. Journey Forge still has three options; later Temper offers avoid Exhaust and Echo costs progressively less maximum HP. Mystery modification prices also decrease. Every price, preview and awarded card matches the actual result.
- Rewrote the Act 3 ending as a resolution for Warden, Vesper and Proxy, and corrected the scene artwork so early endings do not show the secret-boss confrontation. The separate secret-boss revelation is unlocked by the character's tenth recorded Act 3 win, including that tenth win itself. Imported card collections alone no longer unlock it. Old early-revelation saves are repaired.
- Retained the smooth single fade into the ending, all existing artwork, all twelve complete soundtrack streams, and save compatibility. In-game version remains 1.8.6.

Validation: 3,600 standard campaign runs, no stalls, 20.6% Act 3 completion (previous build 20.0%). Loss rates among entrants rose across Acts 1/2/3: 11.1%, 27.4%, 68.0%. Extended-act survivor loss rates still vary; this update keeps the difficulty adjustment modest. Native sanitizer tests cover reward generation, prices, save/resume, enemy damage and all first/ninth/tenth/eleventh ending gates. The exact EBOOT was checked in PPSSPP, including actual reward/Forge actions and complete Normal/Fast ending-frame sequences. Physical PSP testing remains with players.

Install the PSP ZIP or replace `PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT/EBOOT.PBP`. Keep existing save/account files.
