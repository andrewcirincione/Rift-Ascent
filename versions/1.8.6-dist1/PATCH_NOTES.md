# Rift Ascent 1.8.6-dist1 — distribution edition

A separate free distribution ruleset, installed as `PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT_DIST`.
The existing development edition and its saves remain separate.

- Reengineered all 394 card slots, 94 relic contracts and 26 potion contracts
  with new names and written rules. Ten consistently underchosen rewards received
  additional setup, draw, defense or multi-hit roles after simulation review.
- Introduced a shared, capped Echo resource with three distinct character
  generators, optional releases, bounded power triggers and redesigned burdens.
- Preserved the complete original soundtrack and artwork byte-for-byte, as
  confirmed distinct by the owner. Creative provenance was outside this review.
- Preserved five-win secret boss access, the golden A, ten-win card progression,
  the once-only three-character finale and corrected boss finishing transitions.
- Forge choices retain subtle raised highlighting and deck/inventory access.
  Successful potion use does not create a redundant notification box.
- Distribution saves use their own namespace and format. This edition does not
  import development decks whose card slots now have different rules.

See `BALANCE.md` and `VALIDATION.md` for reproducible simulation results, native
rule checks, PSP emulator checks and limitations. The complete PSP ZIP includes
all music, installation instructions and applicable component notices.

This is engineering differentiation, not legal clearance. Being free does not
itself authorize protected copying. The title has not received formal trademark
clearance, and no immunity from claims is promised. Consult `REVIEW.md` before
broad distribution. Historical branches, commits and releases remain unchanged;
share this edition's release assets.
