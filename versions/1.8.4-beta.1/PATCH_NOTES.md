# Rift Ascent 1.8.4 Beta 1

Experimental branch `beta/1.8.4-b1`, based on stable `v1.8.4-r4`. This is a separate prerelease; stable main and the latest stable download remain unchanged.

- Elites and bosses follow their intended cycles without randomly replacing setup turns. Normal enemies retain their existing variation. Half-health boss phases still strengthen attacks, but no longer jump forward in the cycle.
- Stone Giant retains three attacking turns and a reliable Purge. Ossuary Beast, Storm Golem, Pyre Castellan and Rift Behemoth now have deliberate attack sequences ending in Recover. Recover deals no damage and grants no Block, then leaves the enemy Vulnerable for your next turn. The overhead OPEN indicator and intent text identify this opportunity.
- Furnace Maw pressures slow setups with Sear/Burn and Crush, then recovers. Crimson Judge alternates Expose, a light piercing hit, Purge and Recover. Hollow King builds Strength and Block, then uses Flurry and Crush before recovering. The secret Unmaker combines Braced Strike, Dread and Rift Rend before recovery. The Act 7 abomination retains its existing encounter. Selected cycles are written in the ENEMIES inspection tab.
- From Act 2 onward, roughly one quarter of eligible normal/elite spoils offer FORGE INSTEAD OF CARD. Choose an upgrade, a rotating Keen/Ward/Bound modification, or sacrifice 4 maximum HP to Echo an eligible Attack. Echo still costs one extra Energy and plays twice. Only the chosen card reward is replaced; potion rewards and additional Hunt picks remain. You can cancel before confirmation, inspect the result, and save/quit mid-selection. Unavailable choices cannot charge health or trap you. Boss card rewards remain unchanged.
- The end screen is now a run recap: character/mode, acts cleared, room reached, foes defeated, deck composition, relic count, Gold and the lethal enemy/action when applicable. Browse the complete final deck and every relic using the tabs, Square or Triangle. Browsing is read-only. Victory still offers endless mode at the appropriate point; death still retires the run save.

Damage multipliers, enemy health, map distribution, card rarity rules, unlock requirements and the soundtrack are unchanged from r4. A fixed automated player cleared 40/300 fully unlocked campaigns (13.3%), versus 46/300 (15.3%) in stable r4. It chooses card rewards rather than forge alternatives and is not a human-win-rate prediction.

Install the PSP ZIP alongside stable: it uses `PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT_BETA/EBOOT.PBP`. For the standalone binary, rename it EBOOT.PBP and place it in that folder. Beta progress starts separately and uses `BETA184_*` files and `BETA_PROFILE.DAT`; keep these files when updating the beta. Stable `ASCENT18_*` files are not modified, even if both builds are launched from the same folder. The beta is not merged into main.
