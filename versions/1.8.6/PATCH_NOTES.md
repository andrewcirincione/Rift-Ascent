# Rift Ascent 1.8.6

- Three-enemy health numbers now match the normal health font. The player health fill reaches the frame's end and stays within it as maximum health grows.
- Forge selection uses a subtle raised gold highlight consistent with other menus. Deck and inventory browsing remain available.
- The Act 3 boss brings one Crown Tether; the Act 7 boss brings two Unborn Witnesses. Their encounters are tuned to stay close to their former difficulty, with no changes to cutscene artwork.
- Occasional elite pairs introduce the furnace-themed Censer Bailiff, frost-themed Rime Pallbearer and celestial Vowbreaker Seraph. These enemies are exclusive to elite fights.
- A wider Forge pool includes Primed, which places an Attack, Skill or Power in your opening hand within the hand limit. Temper has six possible modifiers, and Echo now accepts several defensive Skills. Each encounter still offers the same number of choices.
- Returning characters gradually recognize past ascents. New story clues, boss dialogue and the special Act 4 encounter explore who built the loop and what binds the three characters to it.
- Existing saves and unlocks migrate automatically, including unfinished Forge/event choices. The PSP startup stack fix and quiet potion feedback are retained.

Validation: native sanitizer checks, matched boss encounters from 6,000 automated baseline campaigns, 3,600 full campaigns on each release, and exact-package PPSSPP controller/visual/cold-boot checks. Full-run wins averaged 20.0% (previous release 21.7%); these are automated policy comparisons, not a prediction of human win rate. All twelve complete soundtrack streams are included. Physical PSP testing remains to be confirmed by players.

Install the PSP ZIP or replace `PSP/GAME/RIFTASCENT/EBOOT.PBP`. Keep existing save/account files.
