# Rift Ascent 1.5

Complete PSP EBOOT with all five music tracks. Keep SAVE.DAT and PROFILE.DAT when replacing your game. Existing runs migrate to the longer acts without losing their cards, relics, potions or current combat.

- Six permanent, individual-card modifications: Echo (play an Attack twice for +1 Energy), Bound (Retain), Keen (+2 base damage per hit), Ward (+4 base Block per card effect), Fleeting (-1 Energy, Exhaust), and Kindled (draw an extra card, Exhaust). Each card has one modification slot, independent of its upgrade. Copies inherit both. Distinct generated seals appear on hand cards and previews; descriptions and live damage/Block calculations include the modification.
- Five new mystery rooms: Echo Crypt, Black Anvil, Cinder Scriptorium, Reliquary Auction and Warden's Bargain. Trade gold, HP or maximum HP for modifications, named relics, card removal or an exact card copy. All 17 mystery rooms now offer two choices and Leave. Card choices show the result before confirmation; canceling spends nothing. Offers remain fixed across saves.
- Four new relics: Ashen Hourglass carries up to 2 unused Energy into the next turn; Ossuary Bell grants 3 Block for each of the first three cards exhausted per turn; Penitent's Needle draws a card after your first self-inflicted HP loss each turn; Duelist's Reliquary grants 1 Energy after you play both an Attack and a Skill. These stack with existing relics and powers, with explicit caps and reset rules.
- Eight new enemies: Gallows Stalker, Candle Wretch, Sepulcher Guard, Thorn Penitent, Veil Duelist, Tomb Cantor, Oathless Lancer and Cathedral Horror. All have original generated gothic artwork, grounded sprites and animated combat presentation.
- Four new enemy actions: Pierce bypasses Block, Sunder removes Block before striking, Flurry attacks three times, and Entomb guards while adding Injury to the combat discard pile. Written intents describe their actual effects.
- Four new player cards: Grave Cleaver removes enemy Block before dealing damage; Dusk Guard gains extra Block against attacking enemies; Mercy's Edge heals you when striking a Weak target; Black Covenant deals damage to all enemies at turn end based on unspent Energy. New cards follow the existing rarity and unowned-card reward weighting.
- Four additional floors in every act: 19 floors including the boss, for a 57-floor normal run. The added route fork offers different room types before rejoining. Shops, mystery rooms and rest sites remain separated, with a final campfire before the boss.
- Rebalanced progression: enemy health increases faster and damage increases more slowly than in 1.4.0. Both continue increasing across acts and endless mode. The early Act I baseline remains close to the previous version.

Validation includes native AddressSanitizer/UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer checks, 7,116 modified-card preview/resolution cases, 1,672,000 scaling cases, 576 independent new-intent cases, all 34 event offers, 40,000 maps and 160,000 meaningful forks, existing damage/Block/Whirlwind/potion/rarity/navigation regressions, and 900 simulated full runs with three heuristic policies. Simulations are calibration evidence, not a prediction of human win rate. The exact complete release EBOOT was tested in PPSSPP with controller transactions and native screenshots; focused runtime checks use deterministic starting fixtures. Physical PSP hardware was not available.

Additional modification audits cover 73,728 physical button combinations, 6,000 shortcut sequences, 9,380 save/resume boundaries and 2,965 static/live card layouts. All six modifications have native-resolution PPSSPP screenshots. Actual controller checks include held confirmation, ignored deck/potion/status shortcuts during modification, exact selected-card changes and a real pause-menu Save & Quit/relaunch.

Full source, generated art, reproducible tests and validation evidence are included in the repository and source archive.
