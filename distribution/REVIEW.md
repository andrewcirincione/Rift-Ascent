# Distribution differentiation review

This is an engineering record, not a legal clearance opinion. The owner requests
free distribution and confirms that the artwork and music are distinct. Those
assets have been retained byte-for-byte; their creative provenance was not audited.

## Changes made

- Replaced the full 394-slot card catalog, including starters, generated cards,
  statuses and all 60 progression unlocks. Names and rules are generated from an
  original declarative catalog. The former character effect catalogs are absent
  from the distribution source snapshot.
- Replaced the inherited character engines with bounded Echo generation and
  release rules. Each character earns Echo differently. Powers use limited turn
  triggers; Wither decays by two. This changes decisions and combinations rather
  than applying a numerical offset to the prior catalog.
- Rebuilt all 94 relic contracts and 26 potion contracts with new names and compound
  effects. Renamed forge traits and updated live descriptions, rewards and shops.
- Preserved this project's story, characters, encounter artwork, music and previous
  progression/visual fixes. The save namespace and format are independent.
- Removed legacy catalogs, old executable dependencies and historical source
  snapshots from distributable archives. The version-control parent history and
  the separate development branch remain intact, as requested.

Names are checked against hashes of the inherited card/relic-name inventory.
This is a regression check for known matches, not an exhaustive search of every
published game. No automated comparison can establish non-infringement.

## Legal framing and remaining limits

The U.S. Copyright Office distinguishes a game's methods of play and ideas from
its protectable literary and pictorial expression. Changing a name or a number
is not a legal safe harbor. The review therefore also changes written rules,
character systems and the overall catalog selection, and avoids claims of
compatibility or affiliation.

Noncommercial distribution is one consideration in fair-use analysis; it does not
automatically permit unauthorized copying. This edition does not rely on being
free as permission to copy protected expression. Mega Crit's content policy
addresses videos, fan merchandise and mods; it should not be treated as blanket
permission to distribute a separate game using its protected content.

The title has not received formal trademark clearance. Copyright analysis also
does not settle trademark confusion, contractual rights, patents or rights in
other jurisdictions. Ownership and licensing of previously supplied code/media
are not established merely by this rewrite. An IP lawyer should review the final
release and the planned distribution before a broad public launch if a legal
risk assessment is required. No promise of avoiding a cease-and-desist is made.

This branch's clean snapshot does not remove older content from the repository's
history or existing releases. Link to the distribution release assets instead of
presenting the whole repository as having been cleared.
