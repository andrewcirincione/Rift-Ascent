# Rift Ascent 1.8.4 revision 4

Based on published v1.8.4-r3, commit 441cbbd671520b21407e137d7f93e0b1447984c0. The in-game version and save struct remain 1.8.4/184. Earlier release tags are preserved.

Build with `PSPDEV=/path/to/pspdev bash qa/v184r4/build.sh`. `pack_art.py` extracts the exact artwork and twelve-track soundtrack from the retained r2 binary; these media are identical to r3. `check_media.py` verifies their byte identity in the complete packaged EBOOT. `release/current.json` identifies the complete binary and required source archive inputs.

Enemy damage first reproduces r3's rounded 1.06 result, then applies the requested 1.30 multiplier. In encounters that began with two foes, the shared encounter helper applies 0.75 before Weak and player defenses. It uses the original encounter count, so killing one foe cannot increase the other's damage. The same helper is used by intent previews, actual hits, and Crane's special Weak modifier. Enemy health and act/floor scaling are unchanged.

Stone Giant now attacks on at least three turns per rotation. The defensive-action replacement chance is 9% instead of 4%. Four new intent IDs cover Bone Bulwark, Chain Lash, Grave Choir and Rift Rend; their effects reuse existing damage, Block, Poison, Vulnerable, Daze and Energy-penalty mechanics. Save validation and rendering recognize the expanded intent range.

Debris keeps its legacy ID, metadata and generated-card behavior for compatibility. `combat_reward_eligible` excludes it from obtainable rewards. Both legacy and account-slot loaders replace only unclaimed Debris offers; owned cards, claimed rewards, offer upgrades and other cards are retained. Keeping the broader legacy eligibility validator permits loading old saves before repairing the offer.

Run `bash qa/v184r4/test.sh` for ASan/UBSan suites and fixture generation. `combat_tuning.c` compares 12,960 combinations with the published r3 resolver, checks all 31 intents and paired/surviving foes, validates Stone Giant's rotation across 1,000 seeds, and exercises the four new actions and their saved combat states. `afterimage.c` retains the mixed-family regression. `reward_tokens.c` checks 54,000 normal/elite/boss offers, real Ancient availability, old-offer migration and generated Debris playability. `proxy_energy.c` verifies six base/upgraded delayed-Energy effects, preview immutability, save/load, multi-card stacking, new-combat reset and Prism Bone. Its optional RIFT_PRIVATE_SAVE_ROOT input is read-only; the player's saves are not included in this repository.

The supplied Proxy snapshot had 6 Energy with a base of 3 and no pending delayed Energy. Three subsequent no-card turns each returned to 3. Hegemony+ and Refine Blade+ are present in that deck and legitimately grant delayed Energy. The checks found no unintended ongoing gain; there is no speculative card nerf or save rewrite.

`tests/campaigns.c` is a deterministic native heuristic player using ordinary starter decks, legal actions, rewards, shops, events, camps and potions. Build with `gcc -O2 -std=gnu11 qa/v184r4/tests/campaigns.c -o /tmp/rift-campaigns`, then run `/tmp/rift-campaigns 100 0 1` for 100 runs per character with full unlocks, or use a final argument of 0 for fresh accounts. Trials stop on Act 3 completion. A 40-action turn cap ends a turn if the policy cycles; 15,000 total actions report a policy stall separately from a loss. Temporary account data is isolated. The policy uses short simulated action lookahead and fixed generic card ratings; it is not human play and is much less effective with Proxy. Seeds and per-run outcomes are retained as CSV. Final full-unlock result: 46/300 wins (15.3%); fresh: 16/300 (5.3%); neither cohort stalled. Unshipped HP-reduction experiments were rejected in favor of the user's accepted 15% level.

`qa_ppsspp.py` exercises the exact complete EBOOT in PPSSPP 1.20.4, PSP 1000/32 MiB mode. Isolated assisted fixtures cover Stone Giant's four turns, each new move, ordinary and boss attacks, a two-enemy encounter, combat save/resume, old Debris-offer migration, and controller-played Hegemony+/Refine Blade+ through delayed Energy and save/resume. It checks streamed-art errors, title music route and nonzero PCM output, and captures 480x272 screenshots. Final observations and binary digest are in `evidence/final-tests.txt`. Software OpenGL, frame skipping/host fast-forward and dummy SDL audio are used; this is targeted emulator testing rather than physical PSP testing.
