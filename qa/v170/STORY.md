# Rift Ascent 1.7 — narrative design

The rift is an engine of obedience disguised as an ascent to heaven. The burning lower wards provide its fuel, the drowned star-court gives that suffering the force of law, and the gilded summit calls the arrangement divine. The same broken key, severed seal and empty crown recur in the three stories. None of this is an exposition screen: players piece it together from their chosen character's memories and the bosses' accusations.

Warden belonged to the furnace wards. He kept a gate closed when an order promised that doing so would save the city. The people behind it burned. He now carries the broken key and the sword of his office upward, seeking the authority that made obedience a virtue. He wants the gate opened, not absolution. He remembers a white-cloaked stranger who passed one child through the bars.

Vesper was a knife of the heavenly choir. Her white cloak once meant that no witness would remain. Ordered to silence a child in the lower wards, she broke the sentence's star-seal and carried the child through a gate whose keeper turned away. Her climb is a return to the institution that called mercy a stain. She intends to sever the command, not merely replace its speaker.

Proxy was made in the blue star-court to rule in another's stead. The judges lent him memories, a crown and the sovereign blade, then cast him into the lower rift when he questioned a sentence. A severed seal gave him one unscripted moment. He suspects the absent sovereign is not asleep at all. He climbs to decide whether a made thing can choose a duty of its own.

The Furnace Maw is the iron prison-engine that consumes the condemned. The Crimson Judge is the authority that turns living names into sentences. The Hollow King is the emptiness at the summit, sustained by the obedience of those below. Boss dialogue and combat use these same three visual identities: chained furnace giant, crimson crowned executioner with a greatblade, hollow crowned monarch with pale mantle and greatsword.

Endless is the unsettling discovery that breaking a throne does not finish the work: the rift repeats the shape of its old command. Each character chooses to keep ascending for their own reason.

## Presentation and controls

Detailed painted gothic art in a centered wide rectangle on black; no pixelation filter, HUD, decorative line work or ambient effects. One short act-entry statement, chosen from ten per character per act (plus ten for acts four onward). Each of nine character/boss pairings has five three-beat exchanges. X advances instantly; text appears fully readable, with no typewriter delay. Entry scenes are silent except for the X advance click. Boss scenes start RiftBoss and preserve its playback into combat. Entry scenes lead directly to the map with RiftMenu. Existing act blessings occur before the entry scene.

The title menu offers Resume Run, Begin Ascent and How to Play. Endless remains the post-victory continuation and existing Endless runs remain resumable.
